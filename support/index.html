Quick answers

Training with PaceIQ

The tips cover the first-launch setup and permissions, pace cues, voices and headphones, and what to expect on the Apple Watch and the iPhone.

How PaceIQ handles your data is in the Privacy Policy.

Subscription

Settings shows your active tier under Current Tier. If it looks wrong, tap Restore Purchases in Settings under About, or at the bottom of the Upgrade screen. Promotional codes go into Redeem Code in the same places.

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. To change or cancel one, open your Apple subscriptions.

Apple Health access

If PaceIQ shows Health Access Needed, the permission lives in the Health app: open it, tap your profile, then Privacy, then Apps, then PaceIQ, and turn on the data. PaceIQ refreshes by itself when you come back.
